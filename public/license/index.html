Each device has its own workspace

Agency devices keep independent local records. They do not automatically synchronize projects, client details or documents with one another.

A backup lets you deliberately transfer records. It does not grant paid access to another browser. In the prepared licensing workflow, releasing an activated browser does not delete its local projects.

Storage and imported images

The workspace has a 50 MiB safety limit, including images and document history. Password-protected encrypted backup exports are available alongside optional readable JSON backups. Keep a recovery copy outside the browser.

Logo and signature imports accept PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP files up to 2 MiB, subject to browser decoding support. Images are normalized for compact document use. SVG and HEIC uploads are not currently supported.

The privacy overview explains local storage, hosting requests and the prepared document-service data flow.

Documents, approvals and payments

Briefs, invoices and credit notes have editable content and document options. An imported signature image is a document element; it does not itself record client approval or establish a remote electronic-signature process.

Approvals and payments are manual records. Record actual client agreement and money received. ScopeLedger does not collect payments, send signing links or approve terms for a client.

Cost calculations are estimates of contribution margin, not net profit or a contractual right to charge. Confirm the original agreement, the additional scope and the client’s approval before starting extra work.
