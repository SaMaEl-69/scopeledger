About ScopeLedger

Clear scope. Considered decisions.

A practical workspace for the moment a client asks for something beyond the original agreement.

Why ScopeLedger exists

A journal section. Another design round. A new integration. A small request can change the cost and timing of a fixed-fee project.

ScopeLedger puts the agreed scope, the new work and its estimated delivery cost into a repeatable decision process. You choose how to handle the change and put the proposed scope, fee and terms into a client brief.

The goal is a clear next step for both sides of the project.

A decision you can follow

  1. Choose a project. Keep each change connected to its project.
  2. Complete its baseline. Record the agreed scope, fee, existing costs and remaining forecast.
  3. Describe the change. Explain the request and the work it adds.
  4. Review costs. Estimate hours, hourly delivery cost and extra expenses.
  5. Choose a fee and response. Charge for additional work, absorb the cost, replace eligible future work or keep the request for later.
  6. Review the brief. Make the deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, fee and approval wording clear.
  7. Export when the service is available. Protected customer PDF exports require a configured service and paid activation.

Read the practical guides for examples of baselines, additional-work pricing and change orders.

Built around fixed-fee work

ScopeLedger is designed for independent professionals, design studios, Webflow shops and agencies that want a deliberate process for additional requests.

Estimates show contribution margin, not net profit. You still need to review the agreement, confirm the scope with the client and use your own accounting and tax judgment.
