Privacy overview

Where your records live, what leaves the browser and how to keep a recovery copy.

Your project records stay in this browser

ScopeLedger saves projects, client details, financial estimates, documents and manual records locally using IndexedDB, the browser’s storage system. The workspace has no customer account or automatic cloud synchronization.

Each browser or device holds its own workspace. Opening ScopeLedger on another device does not bring your records with you. Appearance preferences are also saved in the browser.

The local workspace records are not encrypted by ScopeLedger as a whole. Password protection applies to an encrypted backup you deliberately export.

Keep a recovery copy you control

Choose a password-protected encrypted backup, or an optional plain JSON backup. An encrypted export protects its contents with the password you set. A plain JSON backup contains readable records, so store and share it accordingly.

The application has a 50 MiB workspace safety limit, including records and images. Backups can deduplicate repeated images; an encrypted file’s transport size can differ from the expanded workspace size.

Clearing browser data or deleting the local workspace can remove your work. Export a recovery copy first, keep it outside the browser and protect its password. A backup is a deliberate transfer of records, not a cloud backup or a license activation.

Website requests and hosting

Loading the website and checking service availability still make network requests. Cloudflare hosts the public site and may process request information such as IP address, browser headers, requested paths and timestamps to deliver and protect the service. This is separate from the locally saved project records. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The current Worker logging configuration redacts query strings, and its application error logs do not include request bodies, headers or project data. This does not mean every hosting or network record is absent.

The current application has no integrated advertising or behavioral analytics service. Its local preferences and workspace storage support the product itself.

Activation and document services

The current public deployment is a demo. Purchases, license activation and protected customer PDF exports are not configured. The public demonstration PDFs are sample documents.

When those services are configured, activation communicates with the licensing service. A protected PDF request deliberately sends the client-facing document fields and selected branding to a rendering service. Private delivery hours, internal costs, margins, notes and comparison figures are excluded from that document request.

These prepared service flows describe how the application is designed to work when enabled. Production retention details and final service policies need to be published before paid launch.

Your records and support messages

Use Settings & backup to export, restore or deliberately delete your local workspace. Because the project records are local, support cannot retrieve a lost workspace from a cloud account.

If you email support, the information you choose to send is handled through the support email service. Avoid sending client records, passwords or license keys unless they are needed and you intend to disclose them.

Questions about this overview can be sent to support@scopeledger.site.
