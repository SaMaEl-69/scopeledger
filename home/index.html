How it works

Every extra request gets a decision. Not a reflexive yes.

01 — Describe

Start with your agreement

Choose a project, complete its baseline and describe the change. Enter the hours and costs you expect; ScopeLedger shows their financial effect.

02 — Decide

Swap, Bill or Defer

Review the costs, choose a fee, exchange agreed work or defer the request. You make the decision and record the client’s response.

03 — Review & export

Prepare the client brief

Review the scope, fee and terms. Export a branded PDF, share it through your usual channel, then record written approval before issuing an invoice.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Need help? Email support@scopeledger.site or Open help & support for product guidance.

ScopeLedger helps fixed-fee agencies, freelancers and design studios review a client request against the agreed project scope, estimate its cost, choose a fee and prepare a client brief or invoice. It focuses on the commercial decision behind additional work.

Yes. The free workspace demo includes one sample project. You can inspect the workflow and download the public sample brief and invoice. Purchases, license activation and protected customer PDF exports are not enabled in the current public release.

Individual is planned at $49 once for one activated browser/device. Agency is planned at $99 once for five. Both have the same paid features. Read the license and availability information before purchasing becomes available.

Share the reviewed client document through your usual channel and record the client’s written response in the workspace. Imported signature images and signing lines are supported. An imported image does not record approval, and ScopeLedger does not authenticate signatures or provide remote signing links.

Each activated browser/device has an independent local workspace. The Agency plan is designed for five devices; it does not provide shared accounts or automatic team synchronization. Backups transfer records deliberately.

Project records and document drafts are stored locally in this browser using IndexedDB, with a 50 MiB workspace safety limit. Password-protected backups encrypt the exported copy; local browser records themselves are not fully encrypted. Optional plain JSON backups are readable. See the privacy and data-flow overview.

There are no live capture integrations. Enter or paste the request and compare it with the project baseline. Source text and links provide context; ScopeLedger does not fetch or interpret them automatically.

Start with the agreed deliverables, revision limits, baseline fee and delivery costs. Record exclusions, dependencies and the authorized approver. The project baseline checklist helps you fill in the gaps without inventing an agreement.

Export a password-protected recovery backup from Settings & backup and keep it outside this browser. Keep its password safely. Plain JSON export is also available deliberately. Clearing browser storage can remove the local workspace; browser storage is not a cloud backup.
