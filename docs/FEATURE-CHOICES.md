# Part 2 complementary feature choices

These three additions were selected alongside the mandatory dashboard, calendar, playbook and document-readiness work. Ratings are relative to this agency workflow; they are design judgments, not measured customer research.

| Rank | Improvement                | Use frequency                         | Time saved | Mistakes prevented                                              | Paid usefulness | Learning effort | Maintenance | Ongoing service cost    |
| ---- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ----------- | ----------------------- |
| 1    | Client-response composer   | Each client decision                  | High       | Missing scope, assumptions or approval conditions               | High            | Low             | Low         | None for composing text |
| 2    | Saved decision comparisons | Common commercial choices             | High       | Overwriting the working draft or counting alternatives together | High            | Moderate        | Low         | None                    |
| 3    | Revision differences       | Revised proposals and approval review | Moderate   | Missing a scope, fee, credit or estimate change                 | High            | Low             | Low         | None                    |

The composer expands editable, allowlisted placeholders using the actual client-facing fields and explicit approval state. Unknown placeholders remain visible, and clipboard success is confirmed. It sends nothing and makes no external AI call.

Comparisons save independent terms linked to a change. They calculate against the current decision baseline, clearly labeled as conditional alternatives, without changing the active draft. Archive/recovery and schema-3 backups preserve them. No approval, invoice, payment or committed dashboard total can be inferred from a comparison.

Revision differences compare all scope, classification, response and financial terms against the preceding preserved revision. The historical values remain unchanged. Financial differences stay private in the revision viewer.

A device handoff package was not selected: existing complete backup/restore already transfers a workspace deliberately, while a new selective transfer format would add migration and confidentiality work. Automatic AI writing was not selected because template-based composition meets this task without recurring model charges or another data processor. HTML-to-PDF still requires a running server and Chrome/Chromium; production hosting and Gumroad charges depend on the owner's external setup.
