# Fee, delivery and document verification

Verified locally on 9 October 2026, Asia/Dhaka. This update implements the requested fee choices, delivery extensions, PDF controls, workspace identity behavior and prices. Hosting and seller setup remain outstanding; no real purchase or public deployment was performed.

## Delivered behavior

- **Prices:** Individual USD 48.78 lifetime / one activated browser; Agency USD 99 lifetime / five activated browsers. Both retain the same paid features. Home CTAs, chooser, FAQ, metadata, ROI calculator, workspace activation and operating documentation use these prices. The default illustrative USD 9,000 return is 185×, including the fallback when scripts fail.
- **Fee choices:** Excluding tax enters the net fee and adds the selected tax; including tax enters the final client amount and extracts its net and tax components; custom fee removes the suggested-fee action and uses a manually entered net fee plus the selected tax. Credits follow the selected fee basis. Before-tax amounts drive delivery margins and baseline revenue. Entered inclusive totals stay exact to the cent, including rounding boundaries.
- **Delivery days:** Additional calendar days can be entered in project planning and Choose a fee. Planning is available directly in the guided baseline step. Briefs and invoices show the extension and resulting delivery date where a project deadline exists. Including an approved change adds its days to the project once, alongside net revenue and cost reconciliation. The captured agreed date remains stable after subsequent project timing edits. Payment due dates are independent; deferred requests commit no fee or delivery extension.
- **Document options:** One disclosure contains optional logos, contact details, exclusions, dependencies, assumptions, delivery timing, custom footers and issuer/client signature areas. Hiding a section preserves its saved content. Required issuer/client identity, invoice billing addresses, financial totals and approval/payment requirements remain available. Signature import does not record approval.
- **Agency branding:** Customer briefs, invoices and credits have no “Prepared with ScopeLedger” attribution in the document body or print footer. Their agency identity remains. Public demonstration files retain ScopeLedger letterhead, and demo previews retain their watermark and licensing restrictions. Updated public brief and invoice each occupy one A4 page.
- **Workspace identity/navigation:** The identity card and top avatar open Settings. Uploaded agency logos appear immediately in both locations, persist after reload and fall back to the initial after removal. Active navigation retains its selected background without the left accent stroke.
- **Home detail:** Backup copy now accurately distinguishes password-protected exports from optional readable JSON. Existing unsupported testimonials were not reinstated.

## Compatibility and financial guards

The workspace remains schema 3. Old workspaces, backups, revisions and issued documents without the new fields remain accepted; absent fee/day fields retain excluding-tax, zero-tax and zero-day behavior. Historical document values remain preserved. Material fee, tax or timing edits invalidate unreconciled approval; issuing an invoice still requires approval of the current revision. Reconciliation remains exact-once. Invalid or unfinished numeric drafts stay editable and cannot silently become an agreed amount or valid schedule.

Document visibility is a presentation choice. The server continues to validate an explicit public document schema, omit internal costs/margins/notes, authorize PDF jobs independently and block document scripts and remote resources. Activation keys and session cookies remain outside workspace backups. The restarted local preview remains demo mode with purchases disabled, inactive entitlement and no checkout destinations.
